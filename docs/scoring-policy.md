# Scoring Policy

## Purpose

Scores help partners read candidate fit quickly, but they must not replace human review or imply an automated hiring decision.

## Suggested dimensions

| Dimension | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Role fit | Alignment between partner need, candidate role, stack, and experience. |
| Evidence strength | Quality and confidence of project/portfolio/community evidence. |
| Community signal | Reviewed participation, collaboration, or activity signal. |
| Availability fit | Availability, work type, region, and employment preference fit. |

## Guardrails

- Always show a short explanation next to score/rank.
- Do not expose raw evidence to justify a score.
- Mark low-confidence evidence as review-needed.
- Do not present scores as final hiring decisions.
- Keep scoring behavior covered by tests when possible.
