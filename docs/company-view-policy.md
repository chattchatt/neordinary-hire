# Company View Policy

## Purpose

The company view helps partners decide whether an anonymized candidate is worth a controlled next-step inquiry.

## Allowed content

- Anonymous candidate identifier
- Role and tech stack
- Availability/work preference when not identifying
- Curated project evidence
- Evidence confidence labels
- Score explanation
- Partner-safe summary of community/project activity

## Forbidden content

- Direct contact information
- Discord IDs, handles, raw logs, or message excerpts
- Raw private Drive excerpts or file IDs
- Internal admin notes
- Sensitive matching identifiers
- Any confidential partner/customer source material

## UX copy rules

- Explain why a candidate is relevant.
- State uncertainty clearly when evidence needs review.
- Use human-readable labels rather than opaque scores alone.
- Keep CTA language controlled: `문의하기`, `다음 단계 요청`, or equivalent operator-mediated action.
