# Admin Policy

## Admin responsibility

Admin is the trusted review layer between raw community/project evidence and partner-safe company review.

## Review duties

- Confirm profile completeness.
- Confirm whether Discord/community matching is direct, pending, or uncertain.
- Review Drive evidence confidence and summarize it safely.
- Remove or rewrite raw/private evidence before company exposure.
- Keep internal notes out of partner-facing surfaces.

## Review states

- `ready`: safe for company review.
- `needs evidence`: profile exists but partner value is not yet clear.
- `needs match review`: identity/evidence matching is uncertain.
- `private`: must not be exposed outside admin.

## Escalation

Any suspected privacy leak, secret exposure, or ambiguous consent should block partner demo/release until reviewed.
